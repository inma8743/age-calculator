What is Korean age?

  • International age: checks whether your birthday has passed.
  • Year age: base year minus birth year.
  • Korean counting age: base year minus birth year plus one.

Korea now uses international age for most official purposes, but traditional counting age is still useful for conversation, culture, and older content.
